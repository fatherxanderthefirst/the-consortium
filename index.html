The Code

Know the rules.
Then play the game.

The Consortium is built on competition, freedom, strategy and respect. Every member enters the house knowing where the line is.

01

Enter With Intent

You were not invited to simply observe. Participate. Connect. Compete. Make your presence count.

02

Respect The House

Respect the house, its property, its people and the experience. Leave nothing damaged and leave no one diminished.

03

What Happens Here Stays Here

The Consortium is a private experience. Stories may leave the house. Private moments, conversations and confidential information do not.

04

Play The Game

Every challenge matters. Play fairly, think strategically and accept the outcome.

05

Alliances Are Part Of The Game

Talk. Negotiate. Trade information. Build alliances. Trust is valuable. So is knowing when not to.

06

Expect The Unexpected

The house may test your mind, your social instincts, your strategy and your ability to perform under pressure.

07

Know The Line

Competition is encouraged. Harassment, intimidation, deliberate harm, serious disrespect or destruction of property are not.

08

No Outside Play

Once the house opens, the Consortium becomes its own world. Outside interference does not decide what happens inside.

09

The House Decides

The Game Master and designated Consortium officials have final authority over challenges, scoring, disputes and house decisions.

10

When The Game Ends

When the final is called, the competition is over. No houses. No alliances. No scores. Just the night.

11

Leave Your Mark

Thirty people will enter the house. Make your experience worth remembering.

"Know the rules.
Then make them work for you."
