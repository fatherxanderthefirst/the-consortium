Know
the code.

The Consortium works because everyone understands the difference between playing the game and disrespecting the house.

    Respect the house, the guests and the experience.

  • 02

    Play hard. Keep the competition within the game.

  • 03

    What happens inside the Consortium stays inside the Consortium.

  • 04

    Alliances are allowed. Betrayal is part of the game.

  • 05

    Follow the instructions of the house team at all times.

  • 06

    When the game ends, the competition ends.
